Interpretation: with the state-dependent loss model (eddy ~ f²H²,
pump ~ mdot^2, base overhead ~ Qc) in place of the constant
parasitic_fraction, mu0H_max/frequency/fluid_mdot now carry real
sensitivity in COP_electrical, because raising frequency or field no
longer only raises Qc for free -- it also raises the eddy-current loss
term, producing a genuine efficiency-versus-capacity trade-off
that can be explored during multi-objective optimization.
Caveat: the loss coefficients are calibrated from a small set of
experimental systems (see loss_model.py). The qualitative trends
are physically motivated, while the exact sensitivity magnitudes
should be interpreted in light of the available calibration data.
