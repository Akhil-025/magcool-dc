--- Step 1: peak DeltaT_ad / FWHM vs. sigma_Tc, at Dan'kov et al.'s own three fields ---

--- Step 2: does broadening narrow the field-dependent error pattern already found
    in the pipeline's own Step 1 (core/validation.py's run_validation())? ---

Worst-field |error| vs. sigma_Tc:
  sigma_Tc= 0.0K   worst-field |error|=  48.9%  <- sharp (pre-existing) model
  sigma_Tc= 0.5K   worst-field |error|=  45.6%
  sigma_Tc= 1.0K   worst-field |error|=  42.4%
  sigma_Tc= 2.0K   worst-field |error|=  36.3%
  sigma_Tc= 3.0K   worst-field |error|=  30.7%
  sigma_Tc= 5.0K   worst-field |error|=  20.9%  <- minimum

At 5T, sigma_Tc=5.0K widens the DeltaT_ad(T) FWHM from 31.44K (sharp) to 45.38K.

CONCLUSION: Gaussian Tc-broadening DOES narrow the worst-field |error| against Dan'kov et al.'s three points, from 48.9% (sharp model, sigma_Tc=0) to 20.9% at sigma_Tc=5.0K, driven mainly by the 1T point (+48.9% -> +20.9%). NOTE: sigma_Tc=5.0K is the LARGEST value swept here, and the 1T error is still falling monotonically with sigma at that edge ([48.9, 45.6, 42.4, 36.3, 30.7, 20.9] for sigma=[0.0, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0, 3.0, 5.0] respectively) -- so this sweep has NOT located an interior optimum, only shown the direction of the trend within a physically plausible 0-5K range; a wider sweep (and, ideally, a real digitized dTad(T) curve to fit against, which this repo does not have) would be needed before treating any specific sigma_Tc as a calibrated value rather than a sensitivity finding.
