Electrical-conductivity (sigma_e) correction -- quantified effect on the single-material NSGA-III optimizer's Pareto front
(T_mid=296.00K, pop_size=28, n_gen=12, seed=1; families below are exactly those core.optimize._material_candidates() returns as in-range at this operating point)
====================================================================================================

Before this fix, core.optimize._material_candidates() always left material_sigma_e_S_per_m unset for the intragranular eddy-current channel (core.thermal.intragranular_eddy_power()), which silently fell back to GD_SIGMA_E_S_PER_M=7.6e5 S/m regardless of which family was actually being optimized -- even though particle_diameter_mm is a live NSGA-III design variable (x[6]) feeding directly into this loss term.

RESULT: the correction is real but its magnitude is negligible for every in-range family at this operating point. At representative NSGA-III design points (particle_diameter ~0.5mm, mass ~5kg, mu0H=2T), intragranular_eddy_power() itself is on the order of 1e-3 W, versus Qc on the order of 1e4 W for these families -- a ~7 order-of-magnitude gap. Even the largest single-family swing available (Gd's 7.6e5 S/m down to Ga1-xCMn3+x's 6.0e4 S/m, a 13x change in sigma_e) moves this term by at most a few mW, far below the optimizer's other loss terms and below the resolution these mean-COP/mean-Qc figures can show. So unlike the density fix (which measurably shifted the Pareto front, +6% COP/Qc for La(Fe,Si)13Hy), this correction is physically correct and worth keeping (it's still wrong to silently assume Gd's conductivity for other families), but it does not change any of this repo's existing COP/Qc conclusions at the design points explored here.

Gd5(SixGe1-x)4(-Ga) (tuned, Tc=285.3K):
  sigma_e: 7.60e+05 S/m (Gd fallback, WRONG) -> 7.60e+05 S/m (family's own, literature-sourced)
  mean COP_electrical over 17-point Pareto front: 5.124 -> 5.124 (+0.0%)
  mean Qc_W over 17-point Pareto front: 8928.0 -> 8928.0 (+0.0%)

La(Fe,Si)13Hy (tuned, Tc=285.3K):
  sigma_e: 7.60e+05 S/m (Gd fallback, WRONG) -> 8.00e+05 S/m (family's own, literature-sourced)
  mean COP_electrical over 14-point Pareto front: 9.952 -> 9.952 (+0.0%)
  mean Qc_W over 14-point Pareto front: 30470.6 -> 30470.6 (+0.0%)

Ga1-xCMn3+x (tuned, Tc=295.5K):
  sigma_e: 7.60e+05 S/m (Gd fallback, WRONG) -> 6.00e+04 S/m (family's own, literature-sourced)
  mean COP_electrical over 1-point Pareto front: 0.000 -> 0.000 (+nan%)  (INFEASIBLE -- COP=0 under both settings)
  mean Qc_W over 1-point Pareto front: 0.0 -> 0.0 (+nan%)

Excluded from the table above:
  Gd (fixed): not a correction target -- it IS the fallback value, so before == after by construction.
  Mn1-xCuxCoGe (Mn1-xCuxCoGe): no usable absolute electrical-conductivity literature value was found (sigma_e_S_per_m left None/flagged in core/cascade.py) -- and it falls outside its documented Tc window at T_mid=296.0K regardless, so it isn't even a candidate at this operating point.
  (Mn,Fe)2(P,Si) (MNFEPSI_FAMILY): also outside its documented Tc window at T_mid=296.0K, so it isn't a candidate here either -- see results/material_family_comparison.txt for the (different) operating point where it IS in-range.

Note on Ga1-xCMn3+x specifically: this family has the largest, best-grounded correction of any family (6.0e4 S/m vs Gd's 7.6e5 S/m -- ~13x lower, directly measured on Mn3GaC by Kamishima et al. 2000). If its row above shows COP=0/Qc=0 under both settings, that's a genuine infeasible-design result at this operating point (independently confirmed in an earlier debug run at pop_size=28, and consistent with material_family_comparison.txt's own "INFEASIBLE at this span" finding for the same family) -- not an artifact of this fix, and not evidence the conductivity value itself is wrong.

Deliberately unaddressed: CP_SOLID_GD (specific heat) -- real literature values exist but there is a genuine unresolved methodological question (peak-Cp vs. background-Cp near the transition) that this fix does not attempt to resolve unilaterally.
