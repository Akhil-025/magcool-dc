Notes:
 - MNFEPSI_FAMILY shows the largest correction (density 6370 vs 7900 kg/m^3, ~19% lower): a lower solid density means the SAME regenerator mass occupies a LARGER bed volume, which changes NTU/effectiveness and hence Qc/COP -- direction and magnitude depend on where the design sits on the effectiveness curve.
 - MNCUCOGE_FAMILY (7760 vs 7900, ~2% lower) and GD_FAMILY (7900, unchanged) show the smallest corrections, as expected.
 - GA1XCMN3X_FAMILY is intentionally NOT corrected here (density_kg_m3=None, honesty flag -- no literature density located for this specific alloy) -- both columns are identical for it by construction and it is omitted from the table above.
 - NANOCOMPOSITE_FAMILY inherits LAFESIH_FAMILY's density (it IS three LAFESIH compositions blended), so its correction direction/magnitude matches LAFESIH's.
 - The cascade-comparison correction above is small (<0.1%) at this fixed particle_diameter/geometry -- this operating regime already sits close to NTU-saturated effectiveness, where bed volume (and hence density) barely moves the result. The optimizer table below shows the fix matters far more once particle_diameter/mass are free to explore NTU-sensitive regimes (which NSGA-III actively searches for -- see LAFESIH's ~6-9% COP/Qc shift there), confirming this is a real, geometry-dependent effect and not a fixed constant offset.
