--- Conclusion ---
At the representative operating point, Gd (polycrystalline) (its own Curie temperature sits inside [291.1, 301.1]K) gives the largest passive-regenerator boost: eps 0.829 -> 0.866, COP 12.2283 -> 12.2645 (+0.30%), while La0.7Ca0.3MnO3 (perovskite manganite) (Curie temperature far outside this window) gives +0.00% -- confirming, in this repo's own model, the plan's own framing that the benefit is an alignment effect, not a fixed per-material bonus. Every reported gain is capped by construction at 8% (the illustrative full-effectiveness ceiling -- see the honesty flag above), so these numbers should be read as 'this mechanism could plausibly be worth up to X%, IF the underlying literature-range ceiling holds for a magnetically-augmented regenerator specifically, which has not been separately confirmed' -- not as a validated device-level COP prediction.
