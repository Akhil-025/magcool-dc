CONCLUSION:
Best-performing AMR candidate at the representative point: La(Fe,Si)13Hy (tuned) (1-stage COP_electrical=6.72, Qc=4989.1W).
This does not by itself establish that any AMR candidate beats vapor-compression or
liquid cooling on COP -- see the baselines above and comparison_table.csv/step 4 for
that separate, already-established conclusion. What this table adds is a fair,
apples-to-apples ranking AMONG the giant-MCE material options themselves, including
whether each family's documented tunability window can even reach the ASHRAE point.
