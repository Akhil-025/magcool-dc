    mdot= 0.5000kg/s  COP_ideal=14.149

    mdot= 0.0020kg/s  COP_ideal=15.830
    mdot= 0.0005kg/s  COP_ideal=15.830
  Monotonically non-decreasing as mdot falls (i.e. best at mdot->0)? True
  CONCLUSION: single-objective COP maximization is degenerate in this repo's 2nd-law work model (drives mdot, and Qc with it, toward zero). This module therefore sweeps geometry at a FIXED representative mdot (0.08 kg/s) rather than re-optimizing mdot per geometry the way the paper's own dynamic model does -- a real methodological difference from the paper, stated here rather than hidden.

--- Conclusion ---
Coupling the NTU effectiveness gain to the new geometry-explicit hydraulic
pumping-power term (Tusek et al. 2013 Eqs. 5-7) DOES reproduce a genuine
interior COP optimum vs. packed-bed sphere diameter and parallel-plate
spacing in this repo's own model (Step 3-4) -- confirming the paper's
qualitative finding, which the pre-Phase-7 model structure could not show
even in principle (Step 1). Doing this required fixing mdot at a
representative value rather than re-optimizing it per geometry as the paper
does, because Step 2 found that free COP-only optimization is itself
degenerate in this repo's 2nd-law magnetic-work model (pushes mdot, and Qc
with it, toward zero) -- a genuine, separate limitation, stated rather than
papered over.
The specific optimum found here (packed-bed COP-optimal: 0.5mm;
parallel-plate COP-optimal: 0.1mm) should NOT be expected to match
the paper's Table 3 (0.17mm packed-bed / 0.075mm parallel-plate) exactly:
this repo's operating point (291K/10K span, 2kg, 1Hz, 1.5T, fixed
mdot=0.08kg/s) differs from the paper's own fixed conditions (278-293K/15K
span, 40x10xL mm outer dims, 0.5/3Hz, per-geometry-optimized mdot), and the
pumping power used here is idealized hydraulic power with no pump/motor
efficiency, unlike a real device. The value of this exercise is the
qualitative confirmation that a genuine geometry optimum now exists in the
model where none could before -- not a numerical reproduction of the
paper's own operating point.
