--- Step 1: peak DeltaT_ad / FWHM vs. sigma_Tc, at Dan'kov et al.'s own three fields ---

--- Step 2: does broadening narrow the field-dependent error pattern already found
 in the pipeline's own Step 1 (core/validation.py's run_validation())? ---

Worst-field |error| vs. sigma_Tc:
 sigma_Tc= 0.0K worst-field |error|=  42.3%  <- sharp (pre-existing) model
 sigma_Tc= 0.5K worst-field |error|=  39.2%
 sigma_Tc= 1.0K worst-field |error|=  36.1%
 sigma_Tc= 2.0K worst-field |error|=  30.3%
 sigma_Tc= 3.0K worst-field |error|=  25.0%
 sigma_Tc= 5.0K worst-field |error|=  15.6%  <- minimum

At 5T, sigma_Tc=5.0K widens the DeltaT_ad(T) FWHM from 33.00K (sharp) to 47.05K.

CONCLUSION: Gaussian Tc-broadening DOES narrow the worst-field |error| against Dan'kov et al.'s three points, from 42.3% (sharp model, sigma_Tc=0) to 15.6% at sigma_Tc=5.0K, driven mainly by the 1T point (+42.3% -> +15.6%). NOTE: sigma_Tc=5.0K is the LARGEST value swept here, and the 1T error is still falling monotonically with sigma at that edge ([42.3, 39.2, 36.1, 30.3, 25.0, 15.6] for sigma=[0.0, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0, 3.0, 5.0] respectively) -- so this sweep has NOT located an interior optimum, only shown the direction of the trend within a physically plausible 0-5K range; a wider sweep (and, ideally, a real digitized dTad(T) curve to fit against, which this repo does not have) would be needed before treating any specific sigma_Tc as a calibrated value rather than a sensitivity finding.
